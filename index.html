How to Use This App

Everything you need to know in one place

What Is This App?

This app helps you track your work attendance using a 2-2-2 shift pattern — 2 morning shifts, 2 night shifts, then 2 days off. It repeats every 6 days. Mark when you miss a day, track your streak, view attendance percentage, navigate with swipe gestures, export data as JSON or CSV, and manage everything offline in your browser.

1. Set Your Start Date

Before anything else, you need to tell the app when your cycle begins.

  • Tap the "Set Cycle Start" button above the calendar.
  • The button will change to "Select Day Below...".
  • Now tap any day on the calendar — that day becomes your anchor date.
  • The calendar will instantly color-code every day based on your shift pattern.

You can change your start date anytime by tapping "Change Cycle Start."

2. Understand the Colors

Each day on the calendar is color-coded so you can see your schedule at a glance:

M (Morning) — You work the morning shift
N (Night) — You work the night shift
OFF — You are off duty
MISSED — You marked yourself absent

3. Mark Missed Days

If you missed a morning or night shift, you can mark it on the calendar:

  • Tap any M (Morning) or N (Night) day to mark it as Missed.
  • The day will turn bold red so you can see it clearly.
  • Tap it again to remove the missed mark.
  • You cannot mark OFF days as missed — days off are already rest days.

4. Read Your Stats

The top of the app shows your attendance numbers for the current period:

  • Total Days Present — how many shifts you worked (not marked missed).
  • Attendance % — shown below the total as a percentage of present vs. total working days.
  • Streak — your consecutive present-day streak (backward from today), shown with a fire icon.
  • Missed — how many shifts you missed (shown in red).
  • Days Off — how many OFF days are in this period.

You can also tap the period label to see a quick summary of M / N / OFF / Present / Missed counts for the current period.

5. Navigate Between Periods

Each voucher period runs from the 20th of one month to the 19th of the next. To move between periods:

  • Tap the left arrow (←) to go to the previous period.
  • Tap the right arrow (→) to go to the next period.
  • Swipe left/right on the calendar grid to quickly change periods on mobile.
  • Tap the period label (e.g. "Current Period") to see a popup summary of shift counts.

6. Backup or Restore Your Data

At the bottom of the app, you will find four buttons:

  • Export JSON — downloads a JSON backup file with all your attendance records.
  • Export CSV — downloads a spreadsheet-friendly CSV of every day’s shift and status.
  • Import Data — lets you restore your data from a JSON backup file you exported earlier.
  • Reset All — clears everything and starts fresh. Use this only if you are sure.

Your data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

7. Interactive & Handy Features

  • Copy a date — double-tap any day button to copy its date (YYYY-MM-DD) to your clipboard.
  • Missed popup — when you mark a day missed, a “MISSED” animation flashes on that day.
  • Un-missed popup — tap a missed day again to clear it; a “UNMISSED ✓” animation appears.
  • Anchor flash — when you set or change your cycle start, an “ANCHOR ✓” animation confirms it.
  • Period tooltip — tap the period label to see a quick breakdown of all shift types.
  • Future days — are shown muted and cannot be tapped.

8. Switch Between Light and Dark Mode

Tap the sun/moon toggle at the top-left corner of the app to switch between light and dark themes. Your choice is saved automatically.

9. Where Is My Data Saved?

Everything you enter stays on your phone or computer in what is called "local storage." No internet connection is needed, and no data is sent anywhere. If you clear your browser data or use a different device, your information will not be there — so make sure to export a backup if you need to switch devices.
